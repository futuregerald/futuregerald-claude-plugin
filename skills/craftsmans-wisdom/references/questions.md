# Question Bank

Pick from this bank; don't recite it. Ask one question at a time. If an answer reads like the "weak answer" column, push back once using the "push with" line, then record whatever the user says and move on. The three required questions are marked **(required)**.

## 1. Point of view

| Question | Weak answer sounds like | Push with |
|---|---|---|
| **(required)** Who is this for, specifically? | "Users", "customers", "the team" | "Which one person? Name their role and what they were doing just before they opened this." |
| What do they care about: what they need, not just what they'd ask for? | A feature list | "If it did all of that, what would they still be frustrated by?" |
| **(required)** What does good mean here? | "Clean", "fast", "intuitive", "high quality" | "Write it so the work could fail it. What would a reviewer point at to say it missed?" |
| **(required)** What would the generic version of this look like? | "Not generic" | "Describe the most probable version an AI would give you from a one-line prompt. Which parts of yours are different?" |
| What's the one detail that would make someone say "they thought of that"? | "Polish", "attention to detail" | "Name it. Where is it, and what need does it anticipate?" |
| What should this feel like to use? | "Professional" | "Which value should someone feel in it, and where would they notice it?" |

## 2. Encode it

| Question | Weak answer sounds like | Push with |
|---|---|---|
| Which decisions will someone (or an agent) make on this without you in the room? | "They'll ask me" | "They won't: it'll be built overnight or by someone else. Which three choices are most likely to go wrong?" |
| What's written down that they'll follow? | "It's in my head", "the docs" | "Point to it. Would two people following it build the same thing?" |
| Which of those choices could be a decision tree instead of advice? | "It depends" | "On what? Whatever it depends on is the first branch." |
| Where do right and almost-right look alike, so it needs a do/don't pair? | "They'll know" | "Show me the almost-right version. What tells them apart?" |
| What part of the standard could a script check? | "None, it's taste" | "Which parts are mechanical rather than taste?" |
| Is there a full template or worked example, or only rules about parts? | "We have components" | "Parts that each pass the rules can still add up to an incoherent whole. What shows the whole?" |

## 3. Done ≠ good

| Question | Weak answer sounds like | Push with |
|---|---|---|
| Have you used it the way the user would, end to end? | "I tested it", "the tests pass" | "Tests prove it works. Did you do the user's actual task with it, at their screen size, in their order?" |
| Does it hold together as a whole? | "Each part looks good" | "Read it start to finish. Where does the tone, layout or logic change?" |
| Which decision in it can't you give a reason for? | "None" | "Take three visible choices. Why each one and not another?" |
| Is it fully formed, or just built? | "It's done" | "Are you accepting it because it's fast or because it's good? What's stubbed or 'good enough for now'?" |
| How many versions did you try? | "One, it came out fine" | "The first result is the most probable one. What would a second and third attempt explore?" |

## 4. Ambition and ownership

| Question | Weak answer sounds like | Push with |
|---|---|---|
| What would make this distinctive, not just correct? | "Just ship it" | "AI made this cheaper to build. What would you spend some of those savings on?" |
| *Built:* What strange idea did you drop because it was harder? *Not built:* What strange idea are you tempted to drop? | "Nothing" | "Which version felt risky? What would it take to try it on a branch?" |
| Who maintains this in six months? | "The team", "whoever" | "What breaks first if nobody touches it?" |
| Who is the editor, the person who decides it's good before it ships? | "Code review" | "Code review checks it works. Who checks that it's good, and against what brief?" |
