# Question Bank

Pick from this list rather than reciting it. Ask one question at a time. When an answer sounds like the "weak answer" column, push back once with the line in the "Push with" column, then record what he said and move on.

## 1. Point of view

| Question | Weak answer sounds like | Push with |
|---|---|---|
| Who is this for, specifically? | "Users", "customers", "the team" | "Which one person? Name their role and what they were doing just before they opened this." |
| What do they care about: what they need, not just what they'd ask for? | A feature list | "If it did all of that, what would they still be frustrated by?" |
| What does good mean here? | "Clean", "fast", "intuitive", "high quality" | "Write it so the work could fail it. What would a reviewer point at to say it missed?" |
| What would the generic version of this look like? | "Not generic" | "Describe the most probable version an AI would give you from a one-line prompt. Which parts of yours are different?" |
| What's the one detail that would make someone say "they thought of that"? | "Polish", "attention to detail" | "Name it. Where on the screen or page is it, and what need does it anticipate?" |
| What should this feel like to use? | "Professional" | "Which brand value should show up in it, and where would someone see it?" |

## 2. Encode it

| Question | Weak answer sounds like | Push with |
|---|---|---|
| Which decisions will someone (or an agent) make on this without you in the room? | "They'll ask me" | "They won't: it'll be built overnight or by a teammate. List the three choices most likely to go wrong." |
| What's written down that they'll follow? | "It's in my head", "the docs" | "Point to it. Would two people following it build the same thing?" |
| Which of those choices could be a decision tree instead of advice? | "It depends" | "On what? The answer to 'it depends on what' is the first branch." |
| What part of the standard could a script check? | "None, it's taste" | "Format, required sections, banned phrases, naming? Anything a script can check frees the reviewer for judgment." |
| Is there a full template or worked example, or only rules about parts? | "We have components" | "Parts that each pass the rules can still add up to an incoherent whole. What shows the whole?" |

## 3. Done ≠ good

| Question | Weak answer sounds like | Push with |
|---|---|---|
| Have you used it the way the user would, end to end? | "I tested it", "the tests pass" | "Tests prove it works. Did you do the user's actual task with it, at their screen size, in their order?" |
| Does it hold together as a whole? | "Each part looks good" | "Read it start to finish. Where does the tone, layout or logic change?" |
| Which decision in it can't you give a reason for? | "None" | "Pick three visible choices: a colour, a word, a default. Why that one and not another? (The cup is green, but may as well have been blue.)" |
| Is it fully formed, or just built? | "It's done" | "What's stubbed, hand-waved, or 'good enough for now'? What would finish it?" |
| How many versions did you try? | "One, it came out fine" | "The first result is the most probable one. What would a second and third attempt explore?" |

End this stage by offering `editor-pass` against the brief.

## 4. Ambition and ownership

| Question | Weak answer sounds like | Push with |
|---|---|---|
| What would make this distinctive, not just correct? | "Just ship it" | "AI made this cheaper to build. What would you spend some of those savings on?" |
| What strange idea did you drop because it was harder? | "Nothing" | "Which version felt risky? What would it take to try it in a branch?" |
| Who maintains this in six months? | "Me", "the team" | "Name the person. What breaks first if nobody touches it?" |
| Who is the editor, the person who decides it's good before it ships? | "Code review" | "Code review checks it works. Who checks that it's good, and against what brief?" |
